# Privacy & Child Protection

This platform handles data about children and must be built to protect them.

## Principles

- **Data minimisation.** Collect only what a feature genuinely needs. Prefer local/on-device
  state for anything that does not need a server.
- **Caregiver-mediated accounts for minors.** A child's profile is created and controlled by
  a verified parent or guardian. Children do not hold standalone accounts in early phases.
- **Role-based access.** Learner, parent, guardian, educator, and admin roles gate every
  read and write. A caregiver sees only the learners linked to them.
- **No behavioural ad profiling. Ever.**
- **Consent and deletion.** Caregivers can export and permanently delete a child's data.

## Regulatory alignment

- India **DPDP Rules 2025** — verifiable parental consent for processing a child's data,
  restrictions on tracking and targeted advertising to children.
  <https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190655>
- Design is also mindful of GDPR-K and COPwPA-style requirements for cross-border use.

## The non-negotiable product rule

Technology strengthens human relationships; it never replaces parents, teachers, doctors, or
human connection. No feature may position itself as a substitute for professional care or a
caregiver's judgement.
